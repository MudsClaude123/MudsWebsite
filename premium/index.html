Questions founders ask us first.

Don't see yours? Ask on WhatsApp and an expert will answer.

Which business structure should I choose?
It depends on how many owners you have, whether you plan to raise funding, and how much personal liability you can take on. In a free consultation we compare Private Limited, LLP, One Person Company, partnership and proprietorship for your situation.
What documents do I need to register a company?
Usually identity and address proof for each director and shareholder, and proof of the registered office address. We send you an exact checklist after the consultation, based on the structure you choose.
How long does registration take?
It depends on name approval and government processing. Once your documents are verified, we share an estimated timeline and keep you updated at each stage.
Do you handle compliance after registration?
Yes. We handle annual ROC filings, GST registration and returns, income tax, TDS and PF returns, bookkeeping and event-based filings such as share issues and director changes.
Can you help recover old physical shares?
Yes. Our shares desk traces old holdings, handles transmission and transposition, and supports IEPF claims for unclaimed shares and dividends.
